Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2026 · limnsoft.com

Overview

LIMN ("we", "us", "our") operates limnsoft.com. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our site or reach out about our services.

What We Collect

We may collect your name and email when you contact us, project details shared in correspondence, and basic usage data (browser type, pages visited) via analytics tools. When we make outbound sales calls, we also collect business contact information (business name, phone number, and related details) and, where a call is recorded, the recording and its transcript. We don't collect financial information or anything beyond what's needed to communicate and deliver our services.

How We Use It

Only to respond to inquiries, deliver services, and understand how visitors use our site. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Outbound Calls & SMS

We make outbound sales calls to businesses using an AI voice agent. Calls may be recorded and transcribed for quality assurance and lead qualification. If you give verbal consent during a call, we may send a follow-up SMS containing a booking link. You can opt out of calls or texts at any time by replying STOP to a text or verbally requesting removal during a call, and we honor these requests immediately.

Cookies

We may use cookies to improve your experience. You can disable them in your browser settings.

Third-Party Links

We're not responsible for the privacy practices of any external sites we link to.

Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed. Project correspondence is deleted once it's no longer relevant. Call and lead data (business name, contact info, call summary) is retained only as long as needed for follow-up and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data anytime by emailing contact@limnsoft.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. Continued use of the site means you accept any changes.
